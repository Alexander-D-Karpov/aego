---
code: GMP006
severity: warning
title: "Hurtbox 2D without Health"
example: GMP006.aescene
---

A Hurtbox 2D passes hits to a Health: the one in its Health field, or else the first Health on its node or above it. This one finds none, so hits on it are ignored. It is a warning because the Health may be added later by a script.

**Fix.** Add a Health to the hurtbox node or a parent, or pick one in the Health field.
