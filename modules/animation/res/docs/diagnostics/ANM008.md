---
code: ANM008
severity: warning
title: "A clip reimported with other tracks"
scope: play
---

A clip was imported again during Play and its tracks changed: there are more or fewer, or a track has another node, property or kind. The players of that clip stop, because their place in the old clip means nothing in the new one. A clip with the same tracks and other keys or another length keeps playing from the same point.

**Fix.** Play the clip again, or restart Play.
