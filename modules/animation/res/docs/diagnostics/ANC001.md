---
code: ANC001
severity: error
title: "A condition does not fit its parameter"
scope: asset
---

A transition has a condition on a parameter the controller does not declare, or with an operator its kind does not have: `gt`, `ge`, `lt` and `le` compare `int` and `float` parameters, a `bool` takes `eq` and `ne` or no operator, a `trigger` takes no operator. The controller keeps its last good version.

**Fix.** Declare the parameter with `param "Name" kind=…`, or change the operator to one its kind has.
