---
code: ANM005
severity: warning
title: "The target node of a track is missing"
scope: play
---

The node path of a track does not lead to a node under the player, so the track is off.

**Fix.** Rename the node back, or change the track's path in the clip.

## Details

A renamed or deleted child is the usual cause.

It appears during Play, when the clip is bound to the scene; it is checked again when the hierarchy changes.
