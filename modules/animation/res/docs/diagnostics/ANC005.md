---
code: ANC005
severity: error
title: "Instant transitions form a loop"
scope: asset
---

Transitions without duration, conditions or exit time lead from one state back to it, directly or through other states.

**Fix.** Give one transition of the loop a condition, an `exitTime` or a `duration`.

## Details

Each step would run around the loop until the limit of eight transitions per step. The controller keeps its last good version.
