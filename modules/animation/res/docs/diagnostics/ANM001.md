---
code: ANM001
severity: error
title: "A track cannot be animated"
scope: asset
---

The clip has something the importer cannot read: a kind that does not animate, such as `string`, a key value that does not match the kind, or an unknown block, attribute or field.

**Fix.** Give the track one of the kinds in [Clips](ref:animation/clips), and check that the property path names a field of a component on the target node.

## Details

Nothing is ignored silently: an attribute written as a field inside its block, such as `start` inside `frames`, is refused with the line to write instead (`frames "Slash" start=0.08 {`). The clip keeps its last good version.

During Play the same code is logged when a track's property does not exist on its node or has another type than the track's `kind`; that track is off and the rest of the clip plays.
