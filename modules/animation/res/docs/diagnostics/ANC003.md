---
code: ANC003
severity: error
title: "A transition into its own state without exit time"
scope: asset
---

A transition goes from a state into the same state and has no `exitTime`. Its conditions would restart the state on every step they hold, so the clip never gets past its first frame.

**Fix.** Give the transition an `exitTime`, for example `exitTime=1` to restart after one pass of the clip, or remove it.
