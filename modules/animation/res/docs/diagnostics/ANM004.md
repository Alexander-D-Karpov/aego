---
code: ANM004
severity: warning
title: "Two drivers for one property"
example: ANM004.aescene
---

A track animates a property that something else already drives: another track of an animation, or Motion 2D, which moves the position and rotation of its node.

**Fix.** Animate a child node instead of the node with Motion 2D, or remove one of the two tracks.

## Details

The later track is off, so the property never gets two values in one step.

It appears during Play, when the clip is bound to the scene.
