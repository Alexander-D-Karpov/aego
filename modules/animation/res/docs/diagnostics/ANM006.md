---
code: ANM006
severity: error
title: "A transform track on a physics body"
example: ANM006.aescene
---

A track animates the transform of a node with a physics body that moves on its own. On a dynamic body every transform track is off. On a kinematic body a track with `authority=presentation` is off, because physics draws that body. A static body is moved, with a warning: it is teleported on every step.

It appears during Play, when the body exists.

**Fix.** Animate a kinematic body with `authority=simulation`, so the animation moves it with its velocity; animate a child node for a body that physics moves.
