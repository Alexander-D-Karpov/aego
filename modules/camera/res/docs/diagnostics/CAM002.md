---
code: CAM002
severity: warning
title: "Camera Zone 2D with no camera"
example: CAM002.aescene
---

A Camera Zone 2D switches to a camera while the target is inside its Area.

**Fix.** Pick a virtual camera in Camera, or add a Virtual Camera 2D to the zone node.

## Details

It uses the Camera field or, when that is empty, a Virtual Camera 2D on its own node; with neither it has nothing to switch to.
