---
code: CAM003
severity: warning
title: "A rectangle with no area"
example: CAM003.aescene
---

A Virtual Camera 2D with Use Bounds, or a Camera Zone 2D, has a Bounds or Area rectangle of zero width or height. A camera is then not clamped, and a zone never triggers.

**Fix.** Drag the rectangle handles in the Scene View, or turn Use Bounds off.
