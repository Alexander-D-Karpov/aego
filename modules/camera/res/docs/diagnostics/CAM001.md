---
code: CAM001
severity: warning
title: "Camera Follow 2D or Camera Shake 2D without a virtual camera"
example: CAM001.aescene
---

Camera Follow 2D and Camera Shake 2D act on the Virtual Camera 2D of their own node. Without one they do nothing.

**Fix.** Add a Virtual Camera 2D to the node. Add Component adds it on its own when you add Camera Follow 2D.
