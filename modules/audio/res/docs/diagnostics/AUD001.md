---
code: AUD001
severity: error
title: "The sound file cannot be decoded"
scope: asset
---

An imported `.wav` or `.ogg` is damaged or uses a format the decoder does not read (WAV must be PCM 8, 16, 24 or 32 bit or float; OGG must be Vorbis).

**Fix.** Export the sound again as 16-bit PCM WAV or Ogg Vorbis.

## Details

The clip plays nothing.
