---
code: PHY007
severity: warning
title: "A moving body teleported from Update"
example: PHY007.aescene
---

A script changed the position of a dynamic or kinematic body in `Update`, outside the fixed step.

**Fix.** Move bodies in `FixedUpdate`, with forces, impulses or a velocity, or with `MoveTo` on a kinematic body; set the position directly only to teleport on purpose.

## Details

The change is applied at the next physics step as a teleport: no velocity, no collisions on the way. The example script does this every frame.
