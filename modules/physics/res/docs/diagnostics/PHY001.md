---
code: PHY001
severity: error
title: "A circle or capsule under non-uniform scale"
example: PHY001.aescene
---

Circle and capsule colliders keep their round shape, so the node and its parents must scale X and Y by the same amount. With a scale of (2, 1) the collider cannot follow the drawing and is not created.

**Fix.** Scale the node uniformly and change Radius instead, or use a Box or Polygon collider for a stretched shape.
