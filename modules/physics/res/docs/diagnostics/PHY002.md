---
code: PHY002
severity: error
title: "A collider shape that cannot be built"
example: PHY002.aescene
---

A polygon has fewer than three points, more than eight, points on one line or a concave outline, or a shape has a zero or negative size.

**Fix.** Give a polygon three to eight points that form a convex outline, split a concave shape into several colliders, and keep sizes and radii above zero.

## Details

The collider is not created, and the error blocks Play.
