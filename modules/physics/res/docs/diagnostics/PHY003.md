code: PHY003
severity: error
title: "The body cannot be created"
example: PHY003.aescene
---

The Rigid Body 2D has a value the physics world refuses: a mass override of zero on a dynamic body, negative damping, a layer outside 0..31, or a transform or velocity that is not a number.

**Fix.** Read the message for the field, then give a dynamic body a positive Mass or set Mass Mode to auto.

## Details

The node is drawn but does not take part in physics.
