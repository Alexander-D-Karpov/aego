---
code: PHY004
severity: error
title: "A joint without two bodies"
example: PHY004.aescene
---

A Distance Joint 2D or Revolute Joint 2D connects the body on its node with the body on Target.

**Fix.** Put the joint on a node with a Rigid Body 2D and pick another node with a body in Target.

## Details

One of them is missing, or Target points at the joint's own body, so the joint is not created.
